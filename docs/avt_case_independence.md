# AVT case units and unverified subject independence

Reporting clarification, 22 October 2026. The frozen experiment uses 56 released scan/case IDs, divided into 36 training, eight validation and 12 held-out cases. These partitions contain non-overlapping released IDs. **They have not been verified to contain distinct patients.** No split, model, metric formula, historical value or evaluation timepoint changes with this clarification.

The [original AVT paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8760499/) describes 56 aortas/CTA scans and 20 KiTS, 18 RIDER and 18 Dongyang cases. The inspected paper sections and [official Figshare release](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Aortic_Vessel_Tree_AVT_CTA_Datasets_and_Segmentations/14806362) do not establish one selected scan per person or provide an original-subject crosswalk. A header-only audit of the 56 released CT NRRD files found standard image fields, without patient identifiers or original study/series identifiers. This prevents verifying unique subjects; it also prevents identifying repeat-subject groups from those headers alone. No identity values or per-case header hashes are published here.

The paper cites [TCIA RIDER Lung CT](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/rider-lung-ct/). Its [official legacy description](https://wiki.cancerimagingarchive.net/display/Public/RIDER%2BLung%2BCT) and [original acquisition study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19561260/) describe 32 subjects receiving two chest CT scans within 15 minutes, a design predating AVT. This establishes a repeat-acquisition risk in the cited source. It does **not** identify which source subjects or series became AVT's 18 RIDER cases, demonstrate a particular AVT repeat pair, or prove cross-split subject overlap. Neither leakage nor its absence has been established. No curator crosswalk was found in the inspected accessible material; that does not establish that none exists elsewhere.

Use **case/scan ID** for the observational and resampling unit. Equal-case means, between-case standard deviations and paired within-case method differences are descriptive summaries. Paired case bootstrap intervals are exploratory and conditional on case independence, which remains unverified. Unrecognized same-person dependence could affect uncertainty and training/held-out generalization; pairing methods within a case does not remove dependence between scans. These results cannot establish subject-independent generalization. Resolving this limitation requires a curator-provided subject crosswalk or explicit confirmation of one selected scan per subject.

## Erratum for preserved records

The original [v1 summary](../reports/development_v1_20261022/summary.json) has `statistical_unit: "patient"`. That label is inaccurate: its observations are the eight released validation case IDs, with subject independence unverified. Its `patient_ids`, `patient_mean` and related support keys are retained compatibility labels for case IDs and equal-case summaries. The [machine-readable erratum](../reports/development_v1_20261022/reporting_erratum_20261022.json) binds this correction to the original artifact hashes; all original numbers remain unchanged.

The phrases “All eight patients” in the [v1 readout](../reports/development_v1_20261022/readout.txt) and “training patients only” in the [preprocessing readout](../reports/preprocessing_20261022/readout.txt) likewise refer to released cases. The original files are retained for traceability.

The frozen [analysis protocol](key_ablation_analysis_protocol_20261022.txt), [attribute protocol](attribute_faithfulness_protocol_20261022.txt) and [v2 attribute amendment](attribute_faithfulness_v2_input_amendment_20261022.txt) retain their original bytes. This note corrects their patient-unit interpretation and independence assumption. Existing schema names such as `patient_scope`, `patient_ids` and `all_patient_mean` remain compatibility keys, not evidence of patient identity. Attributed patient wording in cited studies is preserved and is not independent verification of this release's subjects.

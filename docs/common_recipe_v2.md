# Common engineering recipe revision, 22 October 2026

The first two 5,000-update runs learned, but retained substantial errors. Their complete VAL8 measurements are released in [the development record](../reports/development_v1_20261022/readout.txt), including all eight patients and both evaluation timepoints. C reached Dice 73.8430%; the full soft-A platform bridge reached 69.5451%. These models trained on different hardware and software; their difference is not an estimate of the GTA effect. Only two of the seven planned v1 models completed. The remaining v1 jobs were closed before starting, with completed models and their original records retained.

A fixed TRAIN-only diagnostic also found input-size sensitivity. On six centerline-centered patches from three training patients, scoring the same central 96-cube support gave C/A patient-macro Dice 89.756%/84.850% with 96-cube inputs and 86.690%/83.435% with 128-cube inputs. Two CT-selected patches had empty central support; false-positive voxel counts increased with the larger input. Switching only BatchNorm to current-batch statistics did not materially repair overall errors. This small diagnostic does not establish a BatchNorm cause or show that larger-patch retraining will improve validation performance.

The bounded v2 revision makes these common changes before running the complete seven-model comparison:

- Train with 128-cube inputs, matching the existing inference size; retain batch size two and the 1 mm cache.
- Start every model from the same fresh random seed, with 10,000 successful optimizer updates, AdamW initial learning rate 0.0003, weight decay 0.0001, polynomial exponent 0.9 and gradient clipping 12. Evaluate all eight validation patients at updates 5,000 and 10,000; select the checkpoint with greatest mean validation Dice.
- Use Python 3.8.10, PyTorch 1.11.0+cu113, NumPy 1.22.4, SciPy 1.10.1, scikit-image 0.21.0 and SimpleITK 2.3.1 on both training roles. Hardware still differs, so the second role retains a full-A bridge. That bridge is not another random seed.

All 24 frozen model, data, geometry, training and historical source files retain their exact bytes. Attributes, supervision, loss weights, hard and soft condition arithmetic, operators, normalization, sampling, augmentation, split, cache and seed are unchanged. Inference remains 128-cube, batch two, overlap 0.5, threshold 0.5, native-grid restoration and no postprocessing. All final models will be evaluated on one reference environment and device.

This is a combined engineering revision. The nominal input-voxel budget is `(128/96)^3 * (10000/5000) = 4.74074` times v1, and changing patch extent also changes patch-composition targets. A v1-to-v2 difference cannot isolate context size, training duration or software effects. The revision is not evidence of GTA superiority and does not promise a particular score.

Both roles passed synthetic operator and gradient checks and a separate real-TRAIN preflight: seven role-specific models each completed two successful updates, with no AMP retries and approximately 12.52–13.03 GiB peak allocated GPU memory. The two real batches had identical input and target hashes across roles. Preflight models were discarded without checkpoints or held-out-array access. These are implementation checks, not segmentation results. The legacy CUDA runtime reports unavailable deterministic backward implementations for some operators in warning mode; identical seeds and data do not imply bitwise deterministic training.

V2 remains one frozen 36/8/12 patient split, one seed and seven internal jobs: C, soft-A and constant soft-A on the primary role; full-A bridge, scale-only, structure-only and hard anchor on the second. No external architecture is trained. The first comparison is A versus C, supported by A versus matched constant-A; within-role component comparisons are separate. Patch-level global composition does not by itself establish local anatomical targeting.

After models are fixed, validation-only attribute-faithfulness and same-weight availability-only diagnostics will be reported without retuning. Only after all seven model selections are fixed will the held-out TEST12 receive a single explicitly invoked evaluation. Full training recipes are carried into reference-evaluation provenance; the paired analysis rejects missing or mixed recipes after allowing only the method name to differ. No test or larger phase starts automatically.

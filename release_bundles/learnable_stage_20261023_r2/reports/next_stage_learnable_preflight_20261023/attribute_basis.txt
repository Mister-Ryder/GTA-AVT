AVT geometric attributes: definition, support and limits
Completed TRAIN-only audit; no new study arrays or training were used to prepare this summary.

The scale proxy is the annotation-derived physical Euclidean distance transform
(EDT) sampled at resampled Lee skeleton points. Native voxel spacing is accounted
for before sampling to the1mm skeleton grid. It approximates a local inscribed
distance; Lee thinning is distinct from VMTK's Voronoi/maximal-inscribed-ball
construction and is not guaranteed to be a maximal-ball centreline.
Neither this value nor twice it is a standardized clinical cross-sectional
diameter. Physical scale is more directly related to vessel calibre than graph
depth; earlier TRAIN graph analysis found that changing the operational root
could reverse the depth/radius relationship. Cropping and graph cycles further
limit treating root distance or generation as a reliable fine-vessel label.

The2/4/8mm radius boundaries are fixed OPERATIONAL bins, not clinical categories,
optimal thresholds or expert anatomical classes. Soft targets describe skeleton-
node composition, not vessel-volume proportions, physical branch-length weights
or calibrated measurement uncertainty. Targets come from reference annotations
for supervision; deployed conditions are predicted from CT only.

The formal1mm TRAIN36 audit contained55,294 finite positive-radius nodes, with
no nonfinite or nonpositive radius exclusions. Bin counts were8,477 /19,020 /
13,639 /14,158. All36 cases had<=2mm proxy support. The equal-case fine-node
fraction averaged13.6693293% (median11.4236763%, range.6345178–52.1126761%).
Nodes are correlated samples, not55,294 independent vessels or subjects.

Threshold sensitivity: compute each case's fraction of valid radius nodes within
2mm±.25mm or±.5mm, then average cases equally. These means were8.2720943% and
15.7959621%. They are hypothetical threshold-vulnerability intervals, not measured
annotation errors, confidence intervals or evidence to pick a better threshold.

Structure unknown:6,665/55,294 nodes=12.0537491% when node-weighted. The mean of
the36 case fractions was8.5124451% (case range.6866953–41.8794134%). These are
different denominators and must not be substituted. Unknown/rejected skeleton
neighbourhoods remain unknown, not background. Supported junction neighbourhoods
are operational skeleton/arm-support proxies; high unknown fractions and topology
approximation make dynamic junction consumption premature. It remains paused.
The existing structure auxiliary target is held equal in the controlled arms;
this does not establish an independent benefit from that target or junction head.

Local heterogeneity: in the independent descriptive audit, up to256 unique,
GT-centreline-enriched4mm-grid centres/case were sampled with full field-of-view
support. Axis-aligned16mm physical cubes were checked. Among windows containing
valid centreline nodes, the fraction mixing<=2mm and>2mm nodes was computed per
case, then averaged:26.8505066%. It is not natural CT occupancy, an inference
localization rate, or the later model-aligned local-head target population.
This motivates checking local heterogeneity; it does not prove local conditioning
works. The current learnable study uses global128-patch attributes, without a local head.

The radius definition and bins were retained to keep the new study to one change:
learnable condition-feature interaction. No label threshold, unknown rule or
attribute loss was retuned. Full supported/null counts and adverse evidence remain
relevant; head accuracy alone cannot establish useful anatomical correspondence.

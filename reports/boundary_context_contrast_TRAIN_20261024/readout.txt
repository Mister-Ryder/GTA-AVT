English
At unchanged SAAM64 output F0, this diagnostic measures the context-coefficient derivative r=D*dL/dF0 for F0+u*D, rather than F0 multiplicative gain.
D is the predeclared[-,-,+,+] contrast of the four original different-receptive-field responses, projected through the original1x1 weights without bias, retaining the actual uniform1/3 probability factor in FP32. These branches are not established anatomical experts.
Four fixed channel-wise OLS models use FIT24; PROBE12 retains all16 original patches per case and all feature sites. Both direction checks must pass:12 supported, mean>0, at least8 positive and all3 source means>0.
Primary conjunction: False. Original next direction: stop_current_B_and_fixed_context_basis_route.
Passing supports only the prelocked epsilon0.001 finite-step assessment, never training or GTA efficacy. Earlier failed gain routes remain stopped. Absolute energies or descent magnitudes across bases or layers cannot rank architectures. This is a local first-order TRAIN diagnostic using previously trained features and reused cases, not blind generalization or achieved segmentation improvement.

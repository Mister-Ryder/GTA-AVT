Independent classifier pretraining: completed preparation screen
24 October 2026 (China time)

This is classifier preparation, not a segmentation experiment or evidence of useful GTA dynamic conditioning.
Both random-initialized classifier runs reached the predeclared validation-plateau stopping rule. PRIMARY completed12,000 successful updates and selected step8,000; LOCKED_REPLICA completed7,500 and selected step5,000. Neither is labelled a budget-inconclusive run. A plateau under this rule is not proof of a global optimum.

Each run used24 TRAIN cases for gradients, with12 further TRAIN cases for checkpoint selection and diagnostics. The36 cases are source-balanced; the12 probe cases were not new independent test patients. Monitor_A and guard_B are disjoint fixed patch slots on the same12 cases. No VAL8 or TEST12 arrays were used. The complete classifier starts from random initialization; this does not refine the earlier jointly trained classifier. Protected random auxiliary state stays fixed. Selection was fixed prospectively; no alternate checkpoint was substituted after screening.

Original decision: STOP in both runs. Each passed37 of39 checks and failed the same two checks: foreground structure full-KL on monitor_A and guard_B. The complete candidate was not admitted to joint segmentation training. The original failures remain failures.

Partial learning: scale prediction improves over the training-fit prior under the original scale, ordinal, source, presence and variation checks. Structure also learns some conditional composition on average. The latter does not remove the full-distribution errors or establish a universally reliable property.

Secondary structure analysis
For a supported foreground patch whose target empty probability is0, the normalized stored-FP32 proxy obeys
  full_KL_proxy = -log(predicted_nonempty_mass) + conditional_structure_KL.
The proxy is calculated in FP64 after normalizing the recorded FP32 probabilities; it does not reconstruct the original FP64-logit KL. Original KL values remain separately reported. No positive floor was added to model probabilities. Unsupported decomposition would remain unsupported rather than be imputed or dropped.

This is an algebraic diagnostic of recorded probabilities, not a training-gradient decomposition, deletion of valid errors, causal explanation of segmentation gains, or justification to override the original stop rule. The secondary hypothesis was developed using PRIMARY results. Its protocol and reader were frozen before numerical access to the already produced REPLICA predictions; this was not a preprediction freeze or a new independent-data study. The replica vector is true/true/true/false/true (directional conditional improvement,5% marker, availability-cost explanation,all-source nonworsening,8-of12 cases).

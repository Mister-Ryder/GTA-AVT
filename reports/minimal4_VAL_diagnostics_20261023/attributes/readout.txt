Four-model classifier composition diagnostic: mixed/negative validation evidence
The bank has 160 shared patches TOTAL across 8 released cases, producing640 classifier samples for 4 models. It does not have 160 patches per case. Patch128/batch2; all selected checkpoints are at10000 updates. This diagnostic performs classifier-only inference, not segmentation decoder inference.
CT_uniform(64 patches, 8/case) and each of 3 GT-enriched strata(32 patches, 4/case) are reported separately. GT enrichment is not natural prevalence, calibration or a CT-only localization result. Patch fields of view and skeleton support may overlap; they are not independent observations.

Support and unknown targets:
  CT_uniform:53 foreground and 11 true-empty patches. There are 18 patches without centerline support:11 true empty and 7 foreground-unknown. Both heads have 57 valid targets(46 foreground+11 empty). The7 unknown foreground patches have a placeholder empty vector but are excluded from scores; they are not reclassified as true background.
  Each GT-enriched stratum has 32 valid targets per head. Partial unknown structure nodes are excluded before target histograms. No wholly-unknown structure-only patch is present in this bank. The32 case-stratum support records are 8 cases × 4 strata, shared across 4 models, not additional cases.

Actual conditions:
  MAIN_C has trained auxiliary heads, but consumed conditional compositions are null. Its prediction scores do not show that these probabilities regulate its fusion.
  Both A models consume exactly their predicted compositions. Their predicted and consumed score rows are consequently identical.
  SECOND_constant still predicts auxiliary compositions, but consumes uniform scale [1/5] * 5 and structure [1/3] * 3. Its unused classifier predictions must not be mistaken for actual conditioning. Constant-consumed correlations remain null, not zero.

Scores use the original frozen scorer and were exactly reconciled with the saved outputs. MAE averages class probability absolute errors; SSE sums squared class errors per patch; patches are averaged within case, then supported cases equally. Scale has 5 classes and structure3, so raw error magnitudes across heads are not directly comparable. These are not CE, target entropy, gradient strength or calibrated uncertainty.
All legacy patient_* keys and legacy aggregation text in original score files refer to released cases. Independent patient identity is unverified. Spearman is descriptive within each case/stratum, with no p-values, bootstrap or new significance claims. Each GT-enriched correlation uses only 4 patches per case.

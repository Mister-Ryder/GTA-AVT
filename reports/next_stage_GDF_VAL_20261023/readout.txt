GTA-AVT fixed-endpoint GDF auxiliary-supervision pair: VAL8
2026-10-23. Public candidate only; no publication action is performed here.

Decision and attribution

The prospective continuation gate is false. The 2,000-update GDF-supervised
endpoint has nearly unchanged mean Dice and higher mean clDice, with lower GT
centerline recall, higher case Dice dispersion and a worse two-case tail.
Retain all eight cases and the failed gate; this does not support extending the
same recipe or changing weights, threshold, groups or case selection to obtain
a pass. Independent numerical review of the collected original metrics is complete.

Kp and Gp share the same historical constant checkpoint, complete initial tensor
state including the GDF head, seed, optimizer reset and fixed recipe. Both use
constant conditions, q0=s0=0.2 and global attribute supervision weights0.1/0.1.
GDF voxel-MSE weight is0 for Kp and0.1 for Gp, the only scientific arm difference.
Both are fixed successful-update2000 endpoints, not best-validation selections.
Completing this budget is not evidence of optimization convergence.

This is an auxiliary-supervision comparison. It does not test dynamic predicted
conditions, local adaptation or spatial conditioning. Shared-feature learning
and GDF-head learning are not separated by this pair. Inherited supervision in
the common checkpoint is not erased by resetting the optimizer.

KiTS_K18,KiTS_K8,Rider_R10,Rider_R9. Original CT grid, threshold0.5, no connected-

Native Dice is2*TP/max(predicted foreground+truth foreground,1), with no added
epsilon. ASSD is pooled bidirectional six-neighbour boundary-voxel distance, not surfel-
area weighting or an unweighted average of two directional means. HD95 is the
maximum of the two directional95th percentiles. Centerline metrics use Lee
skeleton voxel coverage, not physical arc-length weighting. clDice is their
harmonic mean. No evaluation is re-executed by this release.

Required conjunctive clauses:
  mean Dice delta>=0:                              true
  defined mean clDice delta>=0:                    true
  worst-two mean Dice delta>=0:                    false
  no case Dice decline beyond3pp:                  true
  at least one substantive signal:                 false

A substantive signal means Dice>=+0.3pp OR clDice>=+0.5pp OR worst-two>=+0.5pp
OR Dice SD reduction>=1pp. Values are not rounded before testing. Final=false.
This is a prospective exploratory budget rule, not a significance test or
clinical minimum difference. Case SD is not repeated-training variance.

Scope, provenance and recomputation

One warmstart/seed, eight repeatedly used VAL cases and all source cohorts
represented in TRAIN cannot establish patient-independent generalization,
zero-shot, unknown-source transfer or reduced seed variance. Other U/K runs are
outside this report. The separately failed readout/local oracle remain distinct:
reports/next_stage_diagnostics_20261023/readout.txt.

The field audit and these segmentation endpoints are separate evidence. A field
target's centerline moments are not its whole-voxel loss or gradient contribution.
This release does not add training-curve or per-task gradient measurements.

metadata.json contains the exact scientific recipe, units, gate thresholds and
hash-only bindings for original metric files, endpoint checkpoints, initialization
and archived scientific files. It contains no original execution infrastructure
records. The hashes identify original bytes; they do not alone independently
attest acquisition or evaluation. No raw images, masks, predictions, feature
arrays, trained weights or private drafts are included.

Run: python -I -S recompute.py --check

The standard-library script checks the strict file allowlist, file hashes and
safety patterns, then independently recomputes all10 metric paired means/SD/sign
counts, six geometry summaries/support, worst-two members, FP/FN count totals
and the unchanged continuation gate from the released tables. Nulls remain null.
It compares to expected_summary.json with2e-11*max(1,abs(values)) numerical
tolerance; this is derived-statistic arithmetic, not bit-exact model inference.
All16 original case-metric identities and the33-file scientific archive are
bound; the package does not depend on a private collection or its paired report.

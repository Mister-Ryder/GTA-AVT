  donor_product = f32(a * f32(u_donor))

exactly equal, preserve the exact normally consumed raw c. Otherwise supply
donor_product. Undefined own/donor nonempty composition remains unsupported and
keeps the normal c under the original rule. No tolerance or change threshold is
introduced. Preserved numerical nulls cannot count as actually changed scores.
